Roadmap to the Platinum

⚠ Missable warning — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude is the one potentially missable trophy. In Chapter 1, on a morning after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia meditates on the balcony at Jason's house: talk to her in the kitchen, follow her outside and choose Accept. The moment is gone for good once Chapter 1 ends, and there is no mission replay. It is not yet fully confirmed that this exact meditation is strictly required (there are 18 meditation spots in 10 locations and the 100% checklist needs 15 of 18) — do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.

Step 1 — Finish the story and its optional missions ≈ 60–80 h

Play through all 5 chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Along the way, build habits that pay off later: scan vehicles with the WAiNK app whenever you see a new model (the 100% checklist needs 200 of 277) and study animals through the binoculars (100 of 255 needed). Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation described in the warning above. Nothing else in the story can lock you out of the platinum.

Step 2 — Post-story 100% checklist + challenges ≈ 100 h

After the credits, the 100% checklist unlocks under Progress → Stats → 100% Completion; everything you already did counts toward it. The checklist is only about 60% of true completion — most collectibles are partial — and nothing on it is missable. Complete 70 of the 80 challenges along the way (you may skip the ten hardest), finish all shooting ranges and drone-race locations, and keep scanning vehicles and studying animals until their quotas are met. This step earns the gold trophy A Long Road.

Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks finales + misc cleanup ≈ 10 h

Side-story finales are not part of the 100% checklist, so mop them up now: the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, Bret's hunting finale, and eight full Strangers & Freaks strands for the silver of the same name. Then sweep any leftover one-off trophies — the VCIA jet, the Mile Zero tank, five kills in one Combat Focus — and the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, unlocks when the 54th trophy lands.

Save discipline — there is no mission replay

The game gives you 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission, then rotate the remaining slots for free-roam cleanup. That habit is your only insurance, since finished missions can never be replayed.
